# Privacy Policy for SmartCalc **Effective Date:** November 4, 2026 **Developer:** GeonSoft Studio ## 1. Information Collection SmartCalc does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer or third parties. ## 2. Local Data Storage The application stores calculation history locally on the user's device. This information is used solely to allow users to review their previous calculations. Privacy policy acceptance is also stored locally so users do not need to accept the policy every time they launch the application. ## 3. Data Sharing SmartCalc does not share calculation history or other user information with external servers or third parties. ## 4. Internet Access SmartCalc does not require an internet connection to perform calculations and does not request internet access permission. ## 5. Data Deletion Users can clear their saved calculation history within the application. Uninstalling the application normally removes its private local data, subject to applicable device backup and restore settings. ## 6. Children's Privacy SmartCalc is intended for users aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## 7. Changes to This Policy This privacy policy may be updated if the application's features or data handling practices change. ## 8. Contact For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer using the contact information provided in the application's store listing.
